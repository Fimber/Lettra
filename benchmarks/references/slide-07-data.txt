Read the units before the numbers

Working with tables / Example 07

Distance: 12.5 kilometres
Time: 48 minutes
Average speed: 15.625 kilometres per hour

A number without its unit can be misleading.
Check the column heading and any note below the table.

When rounding a result, keep enough precision for the decision.
Do not add decimal places that were absent from the measurement.

Record the original value before changing its display format.
