Finding a saved document

Help centre / Files and folders

Open the folder where you last saved the document. Sort the list by modification date, then look for the most recent version with the correct name.

If several copies have similar names, open them one at a time and compare the first paragraph. Do not delete the older copies until you have confirmed which version contains your changes.

You can also search for a distinctive phrase from the document. A short phrase is often more useful than a common word.

Example filename: meeting-notes_2026-11-03.txt
