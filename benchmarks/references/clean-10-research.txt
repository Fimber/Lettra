Observations from a small reading study

Working note / 24 participants

Participants read two short passages and answered the same three questions after each passage. The second passage used shorter sentences and wider line spacing.

The median completion time changed from 4.8 minutes to 4.1 minutes. Twenty participants answered all questions correctly in both conditions.

These results describe this small sample only. The order of the passages was fixed, so practice may explain part of the difference.

A follow-up study should vary the order and include readers with a wider range of experience.
