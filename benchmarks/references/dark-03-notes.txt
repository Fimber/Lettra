Keep the useful details

Personal notes / Reading list

A note is easier to use later when it includes a little context. Record where the idea came from and why it mattered at the time.

For a quotation, keep the page number and the exact wording. For a question, write what you have already checked and what remains uncertain.

Small details can save a long search. A title, date or reference number may be enough to find the original source again.
